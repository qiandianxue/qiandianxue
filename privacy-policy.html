This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal data when you use our website, digital services, and mobile applications distributed via Google Play, Apple App Store, and other lawful application marketplaces. This Policy is written to support compliance obligations in multiple jurisdictions and to provide transparent user rights and choices.

1. Scope and Covered Products

  • This Policy applies to all websites, software products, mobile applications, content services, and online commercial modules operated by Beijing Youche Car Service Co., Ltd..

  • When a product has a service-specific notice, that notice applies together with this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide Directly

  • Contact details such as name, email address, company, and communication content.
  • Business account information submitted for support requests and key account cooperation.
  • Purchase and transaction-related information for commercial services, subject to lawful processing limits.

2.2 Data Collected Automatically

  • Device and network data: IP address, app instance identifier, OS version, language setting, and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage data: feature interactions, session timestamps, app version, referral source, and in-app event analytics.
  • Performance logs: error records, latency metrics, and quality monitoring signals.

2.3 Advertising and Monetization Data

  • Ad request and response metadata, ad impressions, clicks, reward completions, and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Advertising identifiers and consent strings where applicable, including Android Advertising ID and iOS identifiers subject to platform permissions.
  • Contextual and limited behavioral signals for ad relevance, frequency capping, and campaign measurement, where legally permitted.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve our services and applications.
  • Deliver customer support, enterprise onboarding, and contractual services.
  • Perform analytics, quality assurance, abuse prevention, fraud detection, and security monitoring.
  • Operate lawful advertising monetization and attribution systems.
  • Comply with legal obligations, law-enforcement requests, dispute resolution, accounting, and audit duties.

  • For other markets (including but not limited to Huawei AppGallery, Samsung Galaxy Store, Xiaomi GetApps, Amazon Appstore, and regional lawful stores), we adapt disclosures and controls according to each marketplace policy framework.
